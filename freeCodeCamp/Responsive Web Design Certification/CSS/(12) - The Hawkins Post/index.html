The Hawkins Post

Something strange is happening in the Hawkins...

Barbara Holland died due to toxic poisons emanating from a local laboratory.

Hawkins officials continue to insist on the accidental nature of Barbara Holland's death, claiming it was the result of a "tragic chemical waste spill." However, reports obtained by our journalists cast doubt on the official claims. According to classified data, toxin levels in the Stingray Quarry area are hundreds of times higher than acceptable levels, but the nature of these substances has yet to be identified by any independent expert. Local residents are complaining of a strange burning odor and increased incidences of dizziness near the area adjacent to the Department of Energy laboratory.

Despite the teenager's death and the parents' growing concern, lab management refuses to grant access to the secret experiment areas. Sheriff Hopper issued a brief statement that "the situation is under control," but hospital sources report that several more people have been admitted with symptoms of severe poisoning from an unknown reagent. Residents are demanding an independent investigation, but their voices seem drowned out by bureaucratic red tape. While authorities debate jurisdiction, the residents of Hawkins are left asking only one question: what exactly is hidden behind the barbed wire fence, and how many more lives will have to pay for this negligence?

11/04/1983
Deleu Denis
