Yuri Gagarin

Who is he?

  • Name: Yuri Alekseyevich Gagarin.
  • Years of life: March 9, 1934 – March 27, 1968.
  • Major achievement: The first person in history to fly into space (April 12, 1961, aboard the Vostok 1 spacecraft, flight duration: 106 minutes).
  • Titles: Hero of the Soviet Union, USSR pilot-cosmonaut, Soviet Air Force Colonel (he received the title out of turn on the day of the flight).

Childhood and Character

  • Origin: Born to a carpenter and a dairy farm worker. His grandfather was a revolutionary worker at the Putilov factory.
  • War: He spent his childhood under Nazi occupation (1941–1943). His family was expelled from their home, and his older brother and sister were deported to Germany. This experience hardened him, but did not embitter him.
  • Education: He began working at an early age. He graduated from a vocational school (as a molder and caster) and an industrial technical school. He also attended an aeroclub, where he made his first solo flight.

The Path to Space

  • Why He Was Chosen: Out of 3,000 applicants, 20 were chosen. Psychologists noted: "He loves heroism, is sincere, pure in soul and body, polite, meticulous to the point of punctuality, has a high intellect, an excellent memory, and is not shy about defending his point of view."
  • Honesty Test: Before the flight, all candidates were given a headache-inducing drug. When asked how they were feeling, five said, "Ready!" while Gagarin honestly admitted, "I've had a headache since this morning." This proved decisive in his favor.

Поехали! (Let's go!)

  • Launch: April 12, 1961, 9:07 AM Moscow time. Before liftoff, he uttered the legendary "Let's go!"
  • Emergency:
    • The spacecraft rose above the calculated point in orbit (risk of delayed return).
    • At the end of the flight, the compartments did not separate immediately, and the capsule tumbled for 10 minutes.
    • During descent, the spacecraft's skin was burning (temperatures up to 5,000 °C), with molten metal flowing down the glass.
    • During ejection, the air valve in the spacesuit did not open immediately, and Gagarin nearly suffocated.
  • Landing: At 10:53 AM in the Saratov region. Local residents (a forester's wife and granddaughter) were the first to greet the cosmonaut.

"Mission of Peace"

  • Nationwide Love: Khrushchev personally greeted him in Moscow, and a three-hour spontaneous rally took place on Red Square.
  • Foreign Visits: He visited approximately 30 countries (Czechoslovakia, England, Bulgaria, Cuba, India, Egypt, and others). In England, Queen Elizabeth II violated etiquette to take a photo with him, calling him a "heavenly" man.
  • Modesty: Despite his fame, he remained humble. His father, a carpenter, told him after his flight, "The main thing is not to get too arrogant."

Personal qualities

  • Honesty: He admitted that journalists make him out to be "super-perfect," but he has weaknesses and mistakes.
  • Caring for others: One night, an 80-year-old man came to him asking him to find his son's grave. Gagarin spent a whole year searching for it in the Kursk region.
  • Family man: Before a flight, he changed his daughter's diapers and joked, "Daddy's going to space, and she's soiling her diapers!"
  • Sense of humor: He could walk through the drainpipe between their balconies to visit his cosmonaut neighbor.

Death (March 27, 1968)

  • Circumstances: He died during a training flight in a MiG-15UTI along with instructor Vladimir Seryogin near the village of Novosyolovo.
  • Significance: For the first time in the USSR, a national day of mourning was declared in memory of a person who was not the head of state.
  • Cause: Long classified. Officially, it was difficult weather conditions and a sudden maneuver. According to Alexei Leonov (declassified later), it was a collision with the wake vortex of another aircraft (a Su-15) that had violated the flight zone.
